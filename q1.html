Emphasis and strengthened text

Tags are enclosed in angle brackets; don't skip heading levels. Gradually increase the number until you're satisfied. For emphasizing and strengthening text, you use the same angle brackets; however, fill the middle with "em" for a bolder, more prominent element, emphasized through italics. for example, Hi Tina was here. The text was italicized.

As for bolding, instead of "em" it would be "strong". By replacing the term "em" with "strong" between the angle brackets, the element will be emphasized through bolding. As shown. Hi Tina was here.

Here are the two side by side. Em: Hi Tina was here, Strong: Hi Tina was here.

the layout should go along these lines:<"em/strong"> text insert <"em/strong">
